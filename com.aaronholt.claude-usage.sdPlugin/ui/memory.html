GB free / used: red below the low line, yellow within 3 GB of it, green above. “Free” counts what Activity Monitor counts: total minus App, Wired and Compressed memory. Once swap reaches the swap line, an amber “SWAP” tag appears top-left.

Memory pressure: macOS's own pressure figure (100 minus the “memory free percentage” that memory_pressure reports), coloured the way Activity Monitor colours its graph. It won't match % used. Swap underneath turns yellow 3 GB before the swap line and red at it.

Banners: the GB views warn when free memory drops below the low line, the pressure view when swap reaches its line or pressure goes critical. Both share a 10-minute quiet period, so one bad moment gives one banner. Tap or click opens Activity Monitor.
