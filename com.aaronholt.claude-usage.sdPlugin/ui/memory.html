Red below the low line, yellow within 3 GB of it, green above. “Free” counts what Activity Monitor counts: total minus App, Wired and Compressed memory. An amber “SWAP” tag appears top-left once swap reaches the swap tag line.

macOS's own pressure figure (100 minus the “memory free percentage” that memory_pressure reports), coloured the way Activity Monitor colours its graph, so it won't match % used. Swap underneath turns yellow 3 GB before the swap line and red at it.

Low-memory and swap banners share a 10-minute quiet period, so one bad moment gives one banner. Tap or click opens Activity Monitor.
